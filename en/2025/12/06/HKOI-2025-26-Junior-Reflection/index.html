Intro

I haven’t talked about this thing before but strange things happened today so lemme talk about it.

So I will talk about the strange things that happened between me and Adam (real).

Fun fact: I am one of the only ones in my past school’s OI team who haven’t seen Adam before.

Why? Because he goes to mainland China for training every summer.

Last summer, me and my friends decided to go to HZ, China to train OI.

However, my MO training haven’t finished, so I had to go there a few days late.

Turns out Adam also went there.

In fact, he was in the same training class as me and my friends.

In fact, he slept in the same dormitory as me and my friends (I wasn’t there yet tho).

(They told me they chatted in Mandarin every night until 2am lol)

But one day he got a fever, and had to go back to HK.

Coincidentally, my MO training ended on the same day, and I went to HZ the next day.

Coincidentally, I got paired on the same bed of Adam’s, and so slept on the same bed Adam had slept.

There are still more things that happened many weeks later about more coincidences that kept me from making friends with him, which I’m not going to discuss here because of length.

Also, because Adam went to Senior group, I did not see him today.

Also, coincidentally, I got the same score as him today.

Also, coincidentally, I wrote a piece of weird code about him during the competition today (see below), before knowing that we got the same score afterwards.

Before The Competition

  • The night before:

I did three junior problems J242, J243, J253, and two easy TFT problems T221, T223 and submitted them all at once.

I got WA in J242, partial pts in J243, but AC in J253, T221, T223. Not bad I guess.

Then I slept. Somehow I couldn’t sleep until pm. Never mind.

  • :

I went to MO training. Just as usual, we did BMT (general & guts) and AIME past paper, wasn’t really hard.

123abc wished me good luck in afternoon’s HKOI, and I proposed to go to Kowloon Tong with him.

I asked Andy if he wanted to come with me, but he said he needed to go home first.

  • :

Wandering in the Another City plaza with 123abc. I didn’t eat breakfast so I bought a little choclate cake to eat.

Then we went back to the metro station, C exit to wait for other juniors.

  • :

Went to CityU with other juniors to meet up with Soso & seniors.

  • :

Seniors seemed like they were not satisfied with the results, later LemonCat told us they had “全軍覆沒” (which is not very true actually).

Sad. And I also start worrying about what fate I will face five hours later.

Also, today morning I got “凶” in the luogu’s “today’s luck” section, and I’m not very happy with it (although I never believed it).

So I thought of all the possibilities that I get HM, and after considering all of them, I realized that the best way to ensure Au/Ag/Cu is to follow my -min deadline discipline.

But anyways, we went to the canteen and EAT. I didn’t know how to order until Soso told me. Soso says my self-managing skills are bad, which is very true.

When eating, I half-jokingly said that I will AC two problems and then water subtasks for the last two.

I didn’t think I would do that in the competition, but turns out I did.

Then Soso said, what if you can’t AC two problems?

I jokingly replied, in Junior? Then stop doing OI lol :)

  • :

Went to the waiting area together. Soso asked Cuigod to tell us what mistakes he had made in today’s Senior. Rip, but it made me even more determined to follow the -min deadline rule.

And then, Soso taught us how to go to the toilet (literally, for strategic purposes). It made quite a lot of sense.

Soso said that people go to the toilet when they AC to show off, which is useless. Instead, they should go to the toilet when they can’t find a solution.

Soso also told me before that I shouldn’t aim for first solve because it is purely useless. Okay.

To my surprise, Mr. Wong Chun was also there (so recognizable cuz so small & cute), and also a red-faced guy (sry) which I later knew to be the very awesome ethening.

Seniors wished us good luck, then we went into the arena.

  • :

Took out my documents, stared at the keyboard to get used to it, then slept.

That’s when suddenly announcement said the competition will begin at , which is secs later, what???

Competition

  • mins: AC T1.

Despite Soso telling us to read the whole paper first (which I did “read” for secs), I figured that T1 was probably the must-AC problem as usual.

So I quickly coded it. Submit. WA. Realize I need to output prefix . Submit. WA. Realize I didn’t handle day equals case. Submit. AC at mins.

  • mins: AC T2.

T2 seems very easy to me so I went on to code it. I realized that I can overkill it using DP so I did. AC at mins.

About this time there was suddenly was an announcement that says that there will be a mock fire alarm. Bruh.

The mock fire alarm then rang many times during the competition which was pretty annoying :P.

Imagine you writing code and then RINGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGG.

  • mins: Think T4.

First I looked at T3. Why is there so many inputs and why is it so long??? T4 looks easier so went on to do T4.

I realized that using a structure with “a middle line and many things sticking to it” is a very good method. But I couldn’t prove that it’s the best.

After thinking for mins, I decided to take a look at T3 first as I haven’t looked at it.

  • mins: Get T3 pts.

After understanding the problem with difficulty, I proceeded to write a solution that used the monoticity of the chosen segments.

WA. Then I simplified the code, and to my surprise, got TLE pts.

However, I figured that the code should run pretty well under almost all data, and was really confused about why I TLEed on the first test case of the last subtask.

After thinking for a few more minutes I figured that I didn’t know how to solve the last subtask, and I also shouldn’t use more time for pts.

Also, my -min deadline has already passed by mins (which is okay cuz a lot of time left), so I proceeded to go to T4.

At this point I knew that I at least had Au/Ag and not HM. So I aim for AK.

Because there’s nothing left in T1, T2 and T3, I decided to delete my deadline and code T4 without considering time.

  • mins: Get T4 pts :P

That’s when the trainers seem very surprised and look strangely at me.

Then a trainer took a front photo of me (I looked at the camera, then the trainer told me to relax and don’t look at the camera).

Then Mr. Wong Chun walked to my back and took a video of me writing code for minutes (???).

I felt pretty confused, but I figured that it must be because I got Junior’s first pts of T3 (which is very right).

But onto T4. As I literally can’t prove my statement (which I thought of earlier), I decided to use the proof-by-AC method.

I decided to hard-code the process. I first use as many s I can with //s (as small as possible) surrounding it, until there are no more of them left.

Then I use as many s I can with s/s surrounding it. Then I use as many s I can with s surrounding it. If there are still too much s, then impossible.

By this point my code was already lines and mins passed. I submit. I get WA, egg points.

I realize that I need to handle the case where there are still s left after using as many of them as possible.

This case literally took me about lines and mins to handle (darn). I submit. I get WA, egg points.

Oh no. But what can I do? I can only continue debugging it. The dark memories of Cuigod’s experience entered my mind. But what can I do?

Then I realize that I need to handle the case where there are still s left after using as many of them as possible.

Despite many similarities with the last case, this case still took me about lines and mins to handle.

I submit. I get WA, this time pts. Darn.

So I decided to generate random data and write a simple checker to check my code. It turned out to be a very good idea that saved the competition.

With this checker, I debugged and found a dozen mistakes or unhandled cases in my code.

Correcting a dozen mistakes took me mins, and now my code has lines. Crazy.

I submit. I get WA, pts. Okay.

Yup Mr. Ethening, that’s how I spent mins getting pts on this darn easy problem.

Anyways. The checker couldn’t find any more mistakes and I couldn’t find any mistakes myself by staring at lines of code. Back to T3.

  • mins: Did nothing :P

I tried to optimize T3 with some more tricks, e.g. Fast IO, some cuts, more cuts. Somehow they all TLE or even WA.

At the last minutes a person sitting two seats to my left started violently hitting his table.

I tried to debug T4 again and literally didn’t know what to do.

Because of my weird connection with Adam (see Intro), I hopelessly wrote the following code at the last minute in despair and submitted:

while (true) printf("I Love You Hopeless\n");

It got “Wall Time Limit Exceeded” and I don’t know what it was.

At the last minute the same person suddenly started yelling and hitting again, but this time seems happy.

Competition end. I lean back and merely see four green boxes on his computer. AK. What.

After the competition

I pack my bag. People started discussing their score immediately. Seems like everyone got and there are many s. What.

I feel like my score was very low. And I didn’t AK. Why didn’t I AC T4? Why didn’t I AC T4? Why didn’t I AC T4?

So I leave the arena feeling not very happy.

Soso, when seeing me, looked very frightened (probably thinking I got HM) and asked me how many points I got.

After I said I got ( in reality) points, Soso laughed and said that he thought I got points.

Ethening came out and talked with me. He questioned why I only got points in T4 when I had mins left.

I told him that I couldn’t debug my code :(, my solution was just too bad.

So ethening told me to write the reflection clearly. So here it is :)

It turns out Sunny got almost the same score as 123abc, congrats!

So we talked for a while and then went to the metro station. Then we found out that the results were out.

What?????? I got Gold First??????

About how the galaxy twisted and turned for me to get Gold First (pure insane luck):

  • The only three person that scored higher than me turned out to be exactly those three unofficial scores.

  • The th subtask of T4 is just points lower than the th subtask of T3, which led my friend Archso to score points lower than me.

  • All of those who solved T4 (officially) only got pts in T3 (why? idk.)

But anyways, I feel a lot better knowing that I got Gold First, although I am still very unsatisfied with my performance of T4.

I am still rank in my opinion :P

Coincidentally, I got the same score as Adam (I am one rank higher yippee XD).

Soso was surprised that Andy get silver (possibly bronze top) without doing OI problems, lol.

Also, the guy who AKed stole my T3 highest score at the last minute, sad.

Also, turns out that we got all of the “firsts”, e.g. Gold first, Silver first, Bronze first, HM first. That means we are “first” I guess?

Also, Doraemon got senior gold #7. Congrats!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (So strong)

We went back to PCMS’s computer room. Then I used minutes to find a solution of T4 that used only lines.

I am so dumb. I am so dumb. I am so dumb.

I take a look at my T3 submission. What. The problemsetter hacked my solution on purpose. Darn. Darn. Darn.

We calculated the school’s grand prize and found that our school got rd, trailing nd by only point. Soso cursed. XD

We then walked to Mong Kok to eat “Clear Soup Cow Meat” (idk lmao).

We talked about girls, gays, femboys, etc. Seems like I was one of the only ones who didn’t get accused of being gay :)

Soso told me that there will be money prizes for getting awards, yayyyyyy.

Thank you so much Doraemon for paying for dinner! (I should’ve also payed cuz Gold but I only had money in my bag :( )

Summary

What I did good:

  • Gold first yayyyyyy (although pure luck)

  • I ACed T1 and T2 in a reasonable minutes, which is pretty fast.

  • I got T3 pts very quickly, and then correctly proceeded to go onto T4.

What I did bad:

  • I didn’t AK.

  • I didn’t AC T4.

About T4:

Usually I’m pretty good at constructive problems, and I have made tons of constructive problems myself.

However, I literally FORGOT that T4 was a constructive problem.

Also, it was a very “loose” constructive problem, because there are tons of ways you can implement it.

Next time, I need to:

  • Think about the estimated length of the code (I’m bad at estimating this, need to practice)

  • Think is there any better way to implement the same solution?

  • Be smarter and go to the toilet if my code exceeds lines.

(And then I can Ctrl+A Delete and then write again lol)

Sometimes taking a step back from my thought process just really matters a lot.

Therefore, I’m also going to modify my -min deadline:

  • If I am stuck on a problem for minutes, then switch to next problem.

Add:

  • If I sense that my code is exceeding lines, then switch to next problem.

  • If there is no next problem, then go to the toilet.

Afterwords

I don’t know what to write, but one day I will make friends with Adam.

Don’t be sad Mr. Wong, we already did better than FireWater XD.

The End
