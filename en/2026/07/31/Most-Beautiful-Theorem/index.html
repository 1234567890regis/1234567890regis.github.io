On the web, if you search "most beautiful theorem in math", you'll probably end up with Euler's Formula, Fermat's Last Theorem, Basel's Problem, etc.

While I do think that these are beautiful from a perspective of a math enthusiast, most people who doesn't have a mathematical background will surely fail to recognize its beauty.

However, I recently found a theorem in euclidean/projective geometry that is beautiful just by looking at it, named Poncelet's Theorem.
