My Pre-NOI training journal.

Original: Chinese

Translation: Gemini

Preface

The training camp started way before the actual NOI. So I went back to my hometown to train OI together with the JSOIers.

Felt quite slacking. Mini-competitions or solving problems in the morning, slacking off in the afternoon, and upsolving problems at night. The slacking off consisted of qym and me introducing them to GeoGuessr or playing GuessSalt.

One time we spawned in Jiangsu in GeoGuessr, but none of them could recognize it. I said, "How can you guys, as Jiangsu people, not even recognize your own hometown?"

As a result, everyone stared at me, which was very awkward.

Turns out, none of them consider themselves Jiangsu people /xk

NOI Pre-contest Training

Back to HK. Flight to QD. Arrived at QD. Took a photo for GeoGuessr which was instantly solved by CE /bx.

We had mini-comps every day. First place gets yuan, second place gets yuan, and third place gets yuan. However, it was completely obvious that I am too noob, so in the mini-comps, I didn't even make it to the top once.

Every day I could basically only solve T1, and sometimes I couldn't even solve T1. It felt way too difficult.

The food tasted like a piece of junk. Fortunately, I ordered delivery on a few days, which included a successful Crazy Thursday, and also got to drink delicious chicken soup.

Met zhikang for the first time. How can he stably solve black counting problems and black DP problems? But how does he not know how to do interactive problems? Typical OIer behavior.

Also met other gods like cmh, Cubber, aaa_Pigeon, f3e6g4, cdx123456, MatrixGroup, etc. Found out that I am the weakest among everyone.

Successfully introduced people in the computer room to GeoGuessr.

Participated in UNR. Successfully obtained a low score of . Time to retire.

Celebrated my birthday. The cake was delicious, and I watched Nailong and GG Bond with zhikang /bx.

End of the camp. Went to hang out with Albert_Wei, and obtained an SMS math contest team uniform. Got completely partial-ordered by Albert_Wei in both MO and OI.

Met lzy, zhy, and zh in real life. How come everyone is about to retire? It feels a bit sad.

NOI, formally starts.
