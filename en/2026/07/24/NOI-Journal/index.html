My NOI journal.

Original: Chinese

Translation: Gemini

17

I got stomach ache at night, fortunately it got better.

18

HK team contestants met up. Found lzy, zhy, zyj, wjc. Exchanged badges. Obtained badges from zhy, wjc, cdx, the HK team, and DengDuck.

Slack off. Eat food. Felt like a piece of junk.

In the following days, wjc would come over to hang out with me and the HK team from time to time (the reason was that lzy, zhy, and zyj are all Arknights fans, so wjc was isolated XD), and I would also go to hang out with wjc from time to time.

19

Opening ceremony. CCF truly treats children like slaves. dzd's speech was way too explosive. The following is the rough main idea excerpt of dzd's speech:

  • The glory of football shines across the world! Except for China. The problem with Chinese football is that it is too administrative. This is not a problem with the Chinese people; it is a systemic problem! If we let CCF manage Chinese football, things should be a bit better.

Eating food, a piece of junk. Practice contest. How come it's WC, haven't done it before. AKed the preliminary written quiz. How come only three people in the HK team AKed the written quiz? A bit noob.

The HK team played rhythm games, I was isolated awa. So I found wjc to seriously learn how to play Japanese Mahjong. Felt like it wasn't hard. Came back and played rngdle with the HK team. Very fun.

20

Gathered. NOI Day 1.

Looked at the first problem, felt like I didn't really know how to do it. Thought for more than an hour, still didn't really know how to do it. Looked at the second problem, felt like it wasn't hard, write it first before anything else. points secured.

Went back to look at the first problem. Wait, how come ? How can pass? Oh, we can use DP. Finished writing it. Why is it tight on memory? Optimized it a bit, and somehow it passed.

How come there is only one and a half hours left? Used minutes to write Subtask and the AB properties. Looked at the third problem. How come I only know the -point solution? Collapsed.

How did xjq get 254? Seriously orz. Got scolded by Soso awa.

Listened to the solutions. Bruh, why were T2 and T3 both so silly? Why did I spend hours on T1??? Seriously reflect.

points, no score drops.

Slacking off. wjc came to play Puzzles with us. We are truly too strong!

21

Social activities, felt like a piece of junk. Visited the Naval Museum (aka played GeoGuessr). Bruh I rolled on rngdle and was made into a meme.

Found wjc to play Japanese Mahjong. Asked him a problem proposed by xjq, but wjc didn't know how to do it either. So rolled rngdle. Bruh, how did I roll ??? Game over, no RP left.

22

Gathered. NOI Day 2.

Looked at the first problem, at first glance, and then figured out . It is divided into segments, consider wqs binary search. Printed a table, it's not convex, collapsed.

Looked at the second problem, wait, I think I know how to deduce the tree backwards using Prufer sequences, so obtained points via . Felt like the rest was very mysterious, couldn't do it.

Looked at the third problem, DP at first glance. Deduced the DP formula for half an hour, couldn't figure it out.

Went back to look at the first problem, felt like it was highly suitable for a greedy approach. But for some reason my brain was completely mushed, couldn't think of it.

Collapsed.

Bruh, how did xjq get points? Seriously orz gold medalist + national first junior official gold medalist.

Listened to the solutions. Bruh, why was T1 so silly? Didn't discover the conclusion for T2, why? Truly couldn't do T3.

Score check. points, no score drops. A total score of points, collapsed.

Looked at the leaderboard. How did xjq truly get a gold medal? How did wjc miss the gold medal line by -6? How come xjq lost the gold medal again (Note: added the China team but actually shouldn't). How come xjq got the gold medal back again.

Seriously played Puzzles. Played Baba Is You, I am too strong. Learned to sing "Run, Youth", felt a bit silly.

23

Attended the theme activity. The singing was all very explosive. Played GeoGuessr. Award ceremony. Met up with JSOIers and SZOIers in real life. How did the entire SZ team explode?

Seriously learned from zxx to sing "Crossing Bridge". Practiced for a long time and felt like I got it.

Went out for a buffet. Quite delicious, finally it wasn't shit.

Returned to the dorm, played Baba Is You. Slept.

24

Woke up. Took a flight back to HK. Played Japanese Mahjong, I completely destroyed xjq (starting hand waiting for tiles to win with Seven Pairs, then xjq dealt the winning tile in the very first round). Group photo.

NOI, ends here.

And I am truly retired.

In the future, I will likely no longer participate in NOI.

Goodbye.
